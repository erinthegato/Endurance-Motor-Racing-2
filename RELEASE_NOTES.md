## Endurance Motor Racing 2.0

The 2027 FIA World Endurance Championship on the PS Vita: 20 Hypercars (LMH and LMDh) and 20 LMGT3s,
built in code to the 2027 technical regulations, on the nine circuits of the 2027 season.

### Install
1. **EnduranceMotorRacing2.vpk**: install with VitaShell.
2. **EnduranceMotorRacing2-music.zip** (once): copy it to the Vita, open it in VitaShell, select all,
   copy, and paste at the root of `ux0:`. It unpacks to `ux0:data/EnduranceMotorRacing2/DATA/music/`.
   Later updates only need the new VPK.

### In this version
- Two classes on one grid: pick Hypercar or LMGT3 at headquarters; class positions, blue flags, class points
- The 2027 calendar: Qatar 1812 km, Imola, Silverstone, Spa, Le Mans 24 Hours, Sao Paulo, Lone Star Le Mans,
  Fuji, Bahrain 8 Hours
- WEC rules: rolling starts, full-course yellows and slow zones, refuelling, three drivers per car and driver changes
- Full-length races in real time (6 / 8 / 24 hours), in the championship too
- The garage computer: SETUP, WEATHER (forecast with icons) and TIMING (live map and timing) tabs, L / R to switch
- Inters and wets selectable in practice and qualifying
- Team manager mode (Role in the race settings): run both of your cars from the pit wall, with live
  timing, a live track map, a screen per car with its orders, DRIVE to get in at a stop, and a dashcam
- Overall position next to your class position on the HUD and the dash; overall timing with class colours
